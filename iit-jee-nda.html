IIT-JEE

Engineering entrance: JEE Main & Advanced.

JEE Main is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) as a computer-based exam, usually in two sessions a year. It is used for admission to NITs, IIITs and other engineering colleges, and it is the qualifying step for JEE Advanced.

JEE Advanced is conducted once a year by one of the IITs, for admission to the IITs. Both exams test Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics.

  • Full Physics, Chemistry and Maths syllabus for Main and Advanced
  • Daily practice problems and chapter tests
  • Full-length mock tests in our computer-based test center
  • Performance analysis and personal mentoring
  • Board preparation alongside, for students in Class 11 and 12

NDA

Defence career: National Defence Academy.

The NDA & NA exam is conducted by UPSC twice a year for entry into the Army, Navy and Air Force wings of the National Defence Academy. It has a written exam (Mathematics and a General Ability Test) followed by an SSB interview.

We prepare students for the written exam with Maths and General Ability classes, and build the fitness, discipline and confidence that the SSB stage looks for.

  • Mathematics for the NDA written exam
  • General Ability Test: English, General Knowledge and current affairs
  • Regular mock tests in the NDA pattern
  • Physical fitness routine on the campus playground
  • Orientation for the SSB interview stage

NDA at a glance

Conducted by
UPSC (Union Public Service Commission)
How often
Twice a year
Written exam
Mathematics + General Ability Test (English and General Knowledge)
Next stage
SSB interview, then medical examination
Who can apply
Unmarried students in or after Class 12. Army wing: any stream. Air Force and Navy: Physics, Chemistry and Maths in Class 12. Age limits apply.

Age limits, eligibility and dates are set in each UPSC NDA notification. Always check the latest notice; our team helps students with the application.
